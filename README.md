# FNP Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Overview
This project analyzes sales data from FNP (Ferns N Petals), a leading gifting and flower delivery brand, using Microsoft Excel and Power Query. The goal is to uncover actionable insights to improve business decision-making, marketing strategy, and operational planning.

---

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel  
- Power Query  

---

## Key Business Questions Answered
- What are the top-performing product categories and occasions?
- When do most customers place orders?
- Which cities contribute the most to sales volume?
- What seasonal trends can the business leverage?
- How can the business optimize delivery and inventory based on demand?

---

## Key Insights
- **Total Orders:** 1000  
- **Total Revenue:** ₹35,20,984  
- **Top Occasion:** Valentine’s Day  
- **Top Categories:** Cakes, Roses  
- **Most Active Time:** 10 PM – 12 AM  
- **Peak Revenue Months:** March and December  
- **Top Cities:** Hyderabad, Mumbai, Delhi  
- **Best-Selling Products:** Personalized Gifts, Cakes  

---

## Business Impact
- Enabled targeted marketing based on occasion and product performance  
- Improved delivery operations with insights on peak order times  
- Informed seasonal campaign planning based on monthly trends  
- Supported expansion strategy through city-wise sales performance
